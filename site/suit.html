The suit

Climb in, step out and let it work, or paint it and find all six helmets.

Climb in

Pilot from the cockpit or from behind. The suit walks with weight, sprints and lands jumps with a stomp that throws things around. Its hand holds your item and punches for four extra damage.

  • A visor HUD: hull, fuel, compass, clock and a readout per tool, in any colour
  • Pack it up into an item and carry it with you
  • Press E inside for your inventory and the suit's. F swaps tools. A power switch parks it.

A mind of its own

A powered suit with a helmet and nobody inside works for you, by the tool in its socket:

  • Chaingun follows you like a tamed wolf and shoots what attacks you
  • EM Projector guards your home and flings monsters into the sky
  • Mining Drill clears out a hall around its home
  • Chainsaw fells the trees nearby

Rogue suits with red visors attack anything. Beat one for its tool, or reboot its mind with a lightning strike.

Make it yours

The suit is bare polished steel until you right click it with a dye. A wet sponge washes it off. Five more helmet styles hide in structure chests as smithing templates, each a nod to a famous helmet.
