The suit

Piloting it, reading its visor, and making it yours.

Climb in

Pilot the suit from its cockpit or watch it from behind. It walks with real weight, sprints, charges its jump and lands with a stomp that throws everything around it. Your own hand still works: the suit's right hand holds your item, punches with four extra damage and reaches further.

  • Walks through leaves, glass panes and cobwebs; sprinting smashes glass and ice
  • A helmet visor with a terminal HUD: hull, fuel and burn rate, a compass, coordinates and a clock in any colour you like, and each tool's own readout (the TNT launcher shows where its round will land)
  • The firebox burns anything a furnace burns
  • Pack it up into an item and carry it with you
  • Your inventory and the suit's in one screen: press E inside, swap tools onto its arm with F, and a power switch to park it with its fuel in

Make it yours

The suit comes out in bare polished steel. Right click it with any of the 16 dyes to paint its plates, and a wet sponge washes it clean again.

Five helmet styles hide as smithing templates in structure chests, each one a nod to a famous helmet. Find them all.
