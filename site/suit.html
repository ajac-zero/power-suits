The suit

Climb in

Pilot from the cockpit or from behind. It walks with weight, sprints, and lands jumps with a stomp. The suit's hand holds your item and punches for four extra damage.

  • A visor HUD: hull, fuel, compass, clock and a readout per tool, in any colour
  • Pack it up into an item and carry it with you
  • Press E inside for your inventory and the suit's; F swaps tools; a power switch parks it

A mind of its own

A powered suit with a helmet and nobody inside works for you, by its tool:

  • Chaingun: follows you like a tamed wolf and shoots whatever attacks you
  • EM Projector: guards your home and flings monsters into the sky
  • Mining Drill: clears out a hall around its home
  • Chainsaw: fells the trees nearby

Rogue suits with red visors attack anything. Beat one for its tool, or reboot its mind with a lightning strike.

Make it yours

Bare polished steel until you right click it with a dye; a wet sponge washes it off. Five more helmet styles hide in structure chests as smithing templates.
