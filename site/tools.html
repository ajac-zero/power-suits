Tools and engines

What goes in the socket, and what powers the suit.

Power tools

Tools fit the socket on the suit's left arm. Hold right click to use them.

  • Chaingun Tap for heavy shots, hold to spin up a ten-a-second barrage. Eats iron nuggets.
  • EM Projector Grabs items and mobs, holds them floating and throws them.
  • Mining Drill Diamond-tipped, digs a 3x3 face at a time.
  • Chainsaw Cut a trunk and the whole tree comes down.
  • Flamethrower A stream of fire that lights blocks and mobs and cooks food.
  • TNT Launcher A revolver drum of TNT. Each round blows up the moment it hits.
  • Rail Cannon Charge it up and let go: one beam through everything in line.

Engines

The backpack is the engine, and you can swap it. Each one has its own look on the suit.

  • Firebox Burns furnace fuel. Floods and dies when water covers it.
  • Tidal Engine Runs for free under water or in rain; drinks water buckets on land.
  • Star Reactor Feed it a Nether Star and it runs everything, forever.
