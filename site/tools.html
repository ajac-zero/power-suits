Tools and engines

Tools fit the socket on the suit's left arm; hold right click to use them. The backpack is the engine, and you can swap it.

Chaingun

Tap for heavy shots, hold to spin up a ten-a-second barrage. Eats iron nuggets from your inventory.

EM Projector

Grabs items and mobs, holds them floating and throws them.

Mining Drill

Diamond-tipped. Digs a 3x3 face at a time.

Chainsaw

Cut a trunk and the whole tree comes down.

Flamethrower

A stream of fire that lights blocks and mobs and cooks food.

TNT Launcher

A four-round drum of TNT. Each round blows up the moment it hits, and the visor draws its arc.

Rail Cannon

Charge it up and let go: one beam through everything in line.

Engines

Firebox

Burns anything a furnace burns. Floods and dies when water covers it.

Tidal Engine

Runs for free under water or in rain, and drinks water buckets on land.

Star Reactor

Feed it a Nether Star and it runs everything, forever.
