Power Suits is a mod about climbing into a power suit. It gives you maximum protection, extra strength and a socket on its arm for heavy power tools. Put a helmet on and you are immune to fire, frost, poison and drowning, but soaking up all that punishment burns fuel, so keep an eye on the gauge.

Step out and the suit carries on by itself: it can drill, cut trees, guard your home or follow you into a fight. Be careful though. Rogue suits exist, and they aren't friendly. More about the suit.

Free and open source. You need NeoForge, GeckoLib 5 and Minecraft 26.3. How to install it.
