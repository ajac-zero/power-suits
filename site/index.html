Power Suits

For situations where plain old armor isn't enough.

An enterable power suit for Minecraft, with power tools, swappable engines and a mind of its own.

Install Gallery

Minecraft 26.3NeoForgeGeckoLib 5

Craft a suit and climb in: more protection, more strength, and a socket on the arm for heavy power tools. With a helmet on you are immune to fire, frost, poison and drowning, at the cost of fuel.

The suit Cockpit, visor HUD, dyes, helmets, and a mind of its own. Tools & engines Seven tools for the socket, three engines for the back. Gallery Screenshots and key art.

Power Suits is open source under the MIT license: github.com/ajac-zero/power-suits. Bugs and ideas go to the issues.
