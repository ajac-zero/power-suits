Power Armor

For situations where plain old armor isn't enough.

An enterable power suit for Minecraft, with power tools, swappable engines and a mind of its own.

Download Source code

Minecraft 26.3NeoForgeGeckoLib 5MIT

Craft a suit, deploy it and climb in. It gives you maximum protection, extra strength and a socket for heavy power tools. Fit it with a helmet and you become immune to fire, frost, poison and drowning. Soaking up that punishment burns fuel, so keep an eye on the gauge.

Get out and the suit carries on by itself: it can drill, cut trees, guard your home or follow you into a fight. Be careful though... rogue suits exist, and they aren't friendly.

Climb in

Pilot the suit from its cockpit or watch it from behind. It walks with real weight, sprints, charges its jump and lands with a stomp that throws everything around it. Your own hand still works: the suit's right hand holds your item, punches with four extra damage and reaches further.

  • Walks through leaves, glass panes and cobwebs; sprinting smashes glass and ice
  • A fuel gauge by the hotbar: the firebox burns anything a furnace burns
  • Pack it up into an item and carry it with you

Power tools

Tools fit the socket on the suit's left arm. Hold right click to use them.

  • Chaingun Tap for heavy shots, hold to spin up a ten-a-second barrage. Eats iron nuggets.
  • EM Projector Grabs items and mobs, holds them floating and throws them.
  • Mining Drill Diamond-tipped, digs a 3x3 face at a time.
  • Chainsaw Cut a trunk and the whole tree comes down.
  • Flamethrower A stream of fire that lights blocks and mobs and cooks food.
  • TNT Launcher A revolver drum of TNT. Each round blows up the moment it hits.

Engines

The backpack is the engine, and you can swap it. Each one has its own look on the suit.

  • Firebox Burns furnace fuel. Floods and dies when water covers it.
  • Tidal Engine Runs for free under water or in rain; drinks water buckets on land.
  • Star Reactor Feed it a Nether Star and it runs everything, forever.

A mind of its own

A powered suit with its helmet on and nobody inside works for its owner. Its job depends on the tool in its socket:

  • Chaingun: follows you like a tamed wolf and shoots whatever attacks you
  • EM Projector: guards your home and flings monsters into the sky
  • Mining Drill: clears out a hall around its home
  • Chainsaw: fells the trees nearby

Rogue suits with red visors are rare, and they attack anything they see. Defeat one for its tool, or wait for a lightning strike to reboot its mind and claim it.

Make it yours

The suit comes out in bare polished steel. Right click it with any of the 16 dyes to paint its plates, and a wet sponge washes it clean again.

Five helmet styles hide as smithing templates in structure chests, each one a nod to a famous helmet. Find them all.

Install

  1. Install NeoForge for Minecraft 26.3.
  2. Download GeckoLib 5 for NeoForge 26.3 and put it in your mods folder.
  3. Download Power Armor and put the jar next to it.

Latest version: {{version}}. Every version is on the releases page.

The suit is in the creative tab Tools & Utilities. In survival, craft a torso, two arms and two legs and assemble them like a body at a crafting table, then craft a helmet and an engine for it. Controls, recipes and every number are in the guide.

Server and client both need the mod.

Verify your download

Be careful with mods from websites you don't know: malware has spread through Minecraft mods before. You don't have to take our word for this one:

  • Built in public. Every release is built by GitHub from the public source code, by a workflow anyone can read. The release links to its build log.
  • Signed. GitHub signs a record of each build: this file, from this repository, at this commit. With the GitHub CLI, check the jar you downloaded: 
    gh attestation verify powerarmor-{{version}}.jar -R ajac-zero/power-suits
  • Checksum. Each release lists the jar's SHA-256. Compare it with yours: shasum -a 256 powerarmor-{{version}}.jar (macOS, Linux) or certutil -hashfile powerarmor-{{version}}.jar SHA256 (Windows).
  • Rebuild it yourself. The build is repeatable: the same source gives the same file, byte for byte. Check out the release's tag and build it with the Java named in the release notes (Temurin {{java}}) and the reproducible.gradle script (saved anywhere): ./gradlew build --init-script reproducible.gradle. The checksum should match.

What the mod does: it adds the suit, its parts and tools to the game. It makes no network connections of its own, runs no other programs and loads no code from anywhere else. Its only dependencies are NeoForge and GeckoLib.
