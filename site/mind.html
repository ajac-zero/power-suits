A mind of its own

A powered suit with its helmet on and nobody inside works for its owner. Its job depends on the tool in its socket:

  • Chaingun: follows you like a tamed wolf and shoots whatever attacks you
  • EM Projector: guards your home and flings monsters into the sky
  • Mining Drill: clears out a hall around its home
  • Chainsaw: fells the trees nearby

Rogue suits with red visors are rare, and they attack anything they see. Defeat one for its tool, or wait for a lightning strike to reboot its mind and claim it.
